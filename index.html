Praphul Singh

Senior Principal Applied Scientist · Oracle Health AI

How can AI systems improve through experience while remaining understandable and reliable?

I study language models and AI agents through this question, working across agent evolution, reinforcement learning, recurrent and looped models, interpretability, and model editing.

At Oracle Health AI, my current focus is Evolve, a governed platform for the automatic evolution of clinical AI agents. My broader work connects research with clinical workflows and data infrastructure operating at the scale of millions of notes. Across Oracle, I have filed 19 U.S. patent applications.

Outside research, you’ll find me writing, playing cricket, singing, or at the gym. I also love being out in nature or simply spending a quiet evening gazing at the moon.

B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering, IIT Kanpur. Based in the San Francisco Bay Area.

AegisVox: Factorized Privacy and Clinical Preservation for Long-Form Speech ↗

Praphul Singh, Akshat Agarwal, Ratul Ghosh, Aashna Kanuga, Devashish Khatwani, Anurag Dwarakanath, Sri Gadde, Krishnaram Kenthapadi

Separating voice identity and spoken identifiers to examine the tradeoffs between speech privacy and clinical preservation.

Paper ↗PDF ↗
Read summary

A factorized framework uses consistent surrogate voices and identifier replacements. Matched renderer comparisons examine replacement realization, speaker linkage, and clinical preservation separately, showing why stronger performance on one measure need not improve the others.

SEPAgents & evaluationPreprint

ContractEval: Query-Conditioned Execution Matching for Procedural Instruction Conformance ↗

Praphul Singh, Shanu Kumar, Akshat Agarwal, Ganesh Kumar

Making an agent’s procedural obligations explicit, so plausible answers cannot hide skipped checks or incorrect execution paths.

Paper ↗PDF ↗
Read summary

Query-active obligations are matched against responses and execution traces to localize omissions, branch errors, ordering failures, and contract violations. Procedural conformance is treated as a distinct diagnostic target rather than assumed from final-answer quality.

Beyond Endpoint Gains: A Weight-Delta Audit of Medical Specialization ↗

Praphul Singh, Shanu Kumar, Akshat Agarwal

Examining what a specialist model’s parameter update explains and what aggregate benchmark gains leave unresolved.

Paper ↗PDF ↗
Read summary

Paired generalist-to-specialist checkpoint audits distinguish reconstructing benchmark movement from explaining it. Component-family interventions and matched controls test whether gains can be attributed to a particular part of the released weight update.

Clinical AI workflows

I’ve designed and built workflows spanning order entry, diagnosis association, search, and note generation. My current focus is how clinical agents can improve through governed evolution.
